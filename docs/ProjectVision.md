# TrialSense-AI

## Vision

TrialSense-AI is an AI-powered clinical trial intelligence platform designed to simplify the process of finding, evaluating, and recommending suitable clinical trials for patients and healthcare professionals.

The platform combines Natural Language Processing (NLP), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Machine Learning to analyze clinical trial data, patient profiles, and eligibility criteria, providing accurate, explainable, and personalized recommendations.

## Problem Statement

Finding the right clinical trial is difficult because:

- Trial information is scattered across multiple sources.
- Eligibility criteria are lengthy and complex.
- Manual matching is time-consuming.
- Patients often miss suitable opportunities.

## Solution

TrialSense-AI automates trial discovery by:

- Understanding patient medical information.
- Matching patients with relevant trials.
- Explaining why a trial is recommended.
- Highlighting eligibility and exclusion criteria.
- Generating AI-powered summaries of trial details.

## Target Users

- Patients
- Doctors
- Clinical Researchers
- Pharmaceutical Companies
- Hospitals

## Long-Term Goal

Build an intelligent assistant that improves patient recruitment, reduces manual effort, and accelerates clinical research using AI.
